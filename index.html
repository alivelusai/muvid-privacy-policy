Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Thank you for choosing Muvid. This Privacy Policy explains how Muvid handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Muvid is designed with privacy in mind. The application does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal information.

The app does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or work address
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Payment information
  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Browsing history
  • Audio recordings

Music and Video Files

Muvid plays music and videos that are already stored on your device. To show your library, the app reads your audio and video files, along with details such as titles, artists, albums, folders, artwork, and thumbnails. All of this is done locally on your device. Your media files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, Muvid saves the following information only on your device:

  • Your favourite songs
  • Your playlists
  • Your recently played songs
  • Your last playing queue and position, so playback can resume

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for its features:

  • Audio and video access (media / storage): to find and play the music and videos on your device.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep music playing in the background and show playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

These permissions are used solely to provide the app's features and are never used to collect personal information.

Third-Party Services

Muvid does not use third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or data collection services.

Data Sharing

Because Muvid does not collect personal information, no user data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Muvid is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any updates will be published on this page along with the revised effective date.
